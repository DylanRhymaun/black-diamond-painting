Exterior Painting

Beautiful Results. Lasting Protection.

A quality exterior paint job is about much more than choosing the right color. It's about protecting your home, preserving its value, and creating a beautiful finish that will stand up to the elements for years to come.
Whether you're building a new home, completing a renovation, or refreshing your existing exterior, Black Diamond Painting delivers the craftsmanship, preparation, and attention to detail your home deserves.
Here in Vermont, our homes face some of the harshest seasonal conditions, from intense summer sun and heavy rainfall to freezing temperatures, snow, and ice. Proper surface preparation and premium-quality materials are essential to ensuring a durable, long-lasting finish. That's why we use trusted paints, primers, and sealants from industry-leading brands like Benjamin Moore and Sherwin-Williams. We never cut corners when it comes to protecting your investment.

  • Thorough Exterior Cleaning: We begin with a comprehensive soft wash to remove dirt, mildew, algae, and other surface contaminants, creating a clean foundation for proper paint adhesion.
  • Meticulous Surface Preparation: All failing, loose, or peeling paint is carefully scraped and sanded, or power sanded are performed as needed to create a sound, smooth surface ready for painting.
  • Complete Property Protection: Your home and belongings are treated with care. All windows, landscaping, walkways, fixtures, and other surfaces not being painted are properly covered, masked, and protected throughout the project.
  • A Clean and Organized Jobsite: We take pride in maintaining a neat, orderly, and well-managed work environment. Work areas are kept clean, with daily cleanup and debris removal to minimize disruption to your home and property.
  • Premium-Quality Materials: We use professional-grade paints, primers, and sealants from trusted manufacturers. We refuse to compromise on quality by using inexpensive or substandard products. Your home deserves materials that deliver exceptional appearance, performance, and durability.

Interior Painting

Transform Your Space. Elevate Your Home.

A fresh coat of paint can completely transform the look and feel of your home. Whether you're looking to refresh a single room, update your home's interior, or bring your vision to life in a new construction project, Black Diamond Painting delivers the craftsmanship and attention to detail that make all the difference.
From personalized color consultations and traditional brush-and-roll applications to beautifully finished, spray-applied trim packages, we offer a full range of interior painting services tailored to your home and your vision. Our goal is to provide exceptional results while treating your home and belongings with the utmost care and respect.

  • Walls, Ceilings & Trim: Professional painting of walls, ceilings, baseboards, crown molding, window casings, doors, and other interior surfaces.
  • Fine Finish Trim Packages: Smooth, professional spray-applied finishes for doors, trim, cabinetry, and other detailed surfaces.
  • Staining & Clear Coating: Professional staining and polyurethane applications to enhance and protect wood surfaces while showcasing their natural beauty.
  • Drywall Repair & Surface Preparation: Patching, repairing, sanding, and preparing surfaces to ensure a smooth, uniform finish.
  • Dustless Sanding: Dustless sanding equipment and techniques are utilized to minimize airborne dust and help maintain a cleaner, healthier work environment.
  • Color Consultations: Assistance with selecting colors and finishes to complement your home's style, lighting, and overall design.
